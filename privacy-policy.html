1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Kitchen Appliance Repair Experts (operating as "Appliance Experts", accessible via this website). We provide independent doorstep repair, burner servicing, diagnostics, and routine maintenance for household gas stoves, built-in hobs, cooking ranges, and kitchen chimneys across India.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, handling, storage, and legal safeguarding of personal data when you interact with our website, request a phone callback, or submit a technician booking form. We are committed to transparency and to upholding your fundamental right to privacy.

2. Information We Collect Through Forms

When you use our online technician booking form or contact inquiry form, we collect only the minimum required information necessary to dispatch a qualified technician to your doorstep and communicate service status:

  • Full Name: To address you respectfully and verify your service appointment upon the technician's arrival.
  • Contact Phone Number (10 Digits): To send SMS/WhatsApp confirmations, coordinate technician arrival time, and provide urgent safety instructions in case of gas leakage reports.
  • Appliance Category & Fault Description: Details regarding your gas stove type (manual ignition, auto-ignition hob, 3/4/5 burner, cooking range, or chimney) and the reported issue (e.g., low flame, spark malfunction, gas odor) so the technician brings the exact spare parts and diagnostic equipment needed.
  • Geographic Location (City, Area & Pincode): To match your request with our nearest local service technician and determine travel feasibility.
  • Technical & Browsing Data: Standard IP address, browser type, referral URLs, and timestamps automatically transmitted by your web browser for security, spam prevention, and server logging.

3. Legal Protection of Your Data Under Applicable Laws

Explicit Legal Commitment: Yes, we collect your information through our web forms, but your data is strictly protected under relevant privacy and cyber laws. We treat your personal contact details with the highest degree of confidentiality and technical security.

We comply fully with governing statutory regulations and international best practices for data privacy, including:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023): All form submissions are handled strictly with your explicit consent for lawful, specified purposes (doorstep appliance repair fulfillment). You retain the statutory right to withdraw consent, inspect your stored details, or request total data erasure at any time.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 & IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011: We maintain strict physical, technical, and operational safeguards to protect Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) against unauthorized access, alteration, destruction, or disclosure.
  • Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules: We guarantee transparent disclosure of our business identity, contact channels, diagnostic charges, and complaint redressal procedures.
  • Google Ads Privacy & Transparency Compliance: We never collect sensitive financial information (such as credit card numbers or bank passwords) through our lead forms. Our forms serve only for direct repair coordination.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your submitted information is utilized strictly for the following operational reasons:

  1. Scheduling and dispatching our field technician to your specified home or commercial address.
  2. Sending appointment verification alerts and technician tracking updates via phone or WhatsApp.
  3. Providing upfront, transparent cost estimates and issuing GST-compliant invoices and 90-day warranty certificates upon job completion.
  4. Conducting voluntary post-service customer satisfaction checks.
  5. Preventing fraudulent bookings and maintaining site security against automated bots.

5. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing Rules

Zero Data Selling Policy: We do NOT sell, rent, lease, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to telemarketers, credit card companies, or third-party lead brokers under any circumstances.

Your information is accessible solely to:

  • Assigned Certified Field Technicians: For the sole purpose of locating your address and repairing your appliance.
  • Secure Infrastructure Providers: Form processing APIs (such as Web3Forms) that transmit your form inquiry over encrypted TLS connections to our administrative desk.
  • Law Enforcement Authorities: Only when strictly mandated by legal court orders, government warrants, or applicable statutory mandates.

6. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards:

  • 256-Bit SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is secured via high-grade Transport Layer Security encryption.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Only authorized operational personnel have credentials to view customer service requests.
  • Automated Data Purging: Routine booking records that have completed their 90-day service warranty window are periodically archived or permanently anonymized.

7. Cookies, Google Ads & Analytics

Our website utilizes minimal, essential cookies and analytics tags to provide a seamless user experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required to keep our forms, accordions, and mobile navigation functioning smoothly.
  • Google Ads & Conversion Tracking: We run Google Ads campaigns to help homeowners discover rapid doorstep gas stove repairs. Google may use cookies or device identifiers to measure ad conversions (such as a completed phone call or booking form submission). You can manage or disable ad personalization through your Google Ad Settings.

8. Customer Rights & Data Erasure

You have full legal control over your personal data. At any time, you may exercise:

  • Right to Confirmation & Access: Request a copy of the personal details we hold regarding your past repair orders.
  • Right to Correction: Update or rectify outdated telephone numbers, addresses, or appliance records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your phone number and address from our active database.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt out of future follow-up notifications or customer feedback calls.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our grievance desk or call our toll-free line.

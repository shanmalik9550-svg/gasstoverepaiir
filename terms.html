6. Customer Responsibilities & Site Safety

To ensure personal safety and prompt service execution:

  • Gas Safety Protocol: If you suspect an active gas leakage or smell LPG/PNG odor, immediately turn off the main cylinder regulator or gas pipeline valve, ventilate the area, and refrain from switching on electrical lights or ignition sources before the technician arrives.
  • Presence of an Adult: An adult customer representative must be present on site during the entire duration of the technician's inspection and repair.
  • Clear Workspace: Please ensure the cooking counter around the appliance is reasonably cleared of cooking utensils and flammable liquids.

1. Appointment Cancellation Policy

100% Free Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: You can cancel or reschedule your technician booking at zero cost at any time before our technician arrives at your premises.

We recognize that schedules change. To cancel or reschedule an appointment, simply call our 24x7 toll-free helpline at 1800-202-6208 or send a quick WhatsApp message to +91 95993 39483 with your booking reference. No penalties or fees will be charged.

2. Doorstep Diagnostic & Inspection Fee Terms

When a certified technician travels to your location and performs an expert diagnostic inspection on your gas stove, hob, cooking range, or kitchen chimney:

  • If Repair is Approved: The ₹299 diagnostic fee is completely waived and deducted directly from the total repair bill. You pay only for the agreed repair cost.
  • If Repair is Declined: If the customer chooses not to proceed with the repair after the technician has conducted physical diagnosis and provided the transparent quote, the nominal diagnostic visit fee of ₹299 is payable to cover the technician’s fuel, transit time, and specialized inspection work. This visit fee is non-refundable once the diagnostic visit has occurred.

3. 90-Day Warranty Service Rework Policy

Free Re-Service Guarantee: We stand behind our workmanship. If an appliance component repaired or replaced by our technician exhibits the same defect within our 90-Day Doorstep Warranty period, we will dispatch a senior technician to re-inspect and rectify the problem with zero additional labor or parts fee.

Our primary commitment is always to ensure your kitchen appliance works safely and flawlessly. In the rare event that an issue cannot be resolved under the warranty rework procedure, our management will review the case for an appropriate resolution or partial refund of service labor fees.

4. Advance Deposit & Parts Unavailability Refunds

In certain cases involving rare, imported, or discontinued models, a nominal advance deposit may be requested to source specific custom replacement parts:

  • If our procurement team determines that a specific replacement component is permanently obsolete or unavailable in the market, 100% of your advance deposit will be refunded promptly.
  • If you decide to cancel the custom parts procurement order after our team has already purchased a non-returnable specialized OEM part upon your written approval, only the actual part restocking cost may be adjusted, and the remainder refunded.

5. Refund Method & Timeline (3-5 Business Days)

All approved refunds are processed expeditiously and transparently:

  • Digital Payment Refunds: Payments made via UPI, NetBanking, Debit/Credit Card, or digital wallets will be credited back directly to the original bank account or UPI ID used during the transaction.
  • Cash Transactions: For services paid in cash, refunds will be transferred electronically via UPI or NEFT directly to the customer’s verified bank account upon submission of account details.
  • Processing Timeline: Approved refunds will be initiated within 24 to 48 hours and typically reflect in your account within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your banking provider.
